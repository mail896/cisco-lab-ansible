Lab network authorisation

The school and exercise interfaces have separate purposes. The internal NIC
uses the school connection, its default route and DNS. The USB Ethernet
adapter connects to the student's physical network exercise.

NetworkManager's general Polkit write action does not carry a connection
UUID in the checked 1.52.1 source. The classroom group therefore uses a
limited dialog and root broker for changes to the USB lab interface.
See the NetworkManager authorisation and connection-update source:
https://github.com/NetworkManager/NetworkManager/blob/1.52.1/src/core/nm-auth-manager.c#L369
https://github.com/NetworkManager/NetworkManager/blob/1.52.1/src/core/settings/nm-settings-connection.c#L1735

The Unix socket identifies callers through kernel peer credentials. The
broker validates group membership and the active local logind session.
A root-managed manifest identifies the school and lab adapters by profile
and hardware. The dialog cannot choose arbitrary adapters, file paths,
profile identifiers, DNS settings or shell commands.

Lab requests use manual IPv4 and IPv6 addresses and specific routes.
Default routes and routes overlapping protected school networks are
rejected. Validate the real school, DNS and management prefixes in the
manifest when commissioning a new host. A specific route to an unprotected
destination is part of the lab exercise and can redirect that traffic.

The broker serialises application, recovery and login reset. The recovery
loop restores the school path and a missing lab profile, while allowing
students to retain exercise values within their active session. A new
classroom login resets the lab configuration. Review school connectivity,
identity matching, rollback and exercise routes on a pilot client before
extending the configuration to a room.

See ../broker/, ../dialog/ and ../../docs/dashboard.md.
